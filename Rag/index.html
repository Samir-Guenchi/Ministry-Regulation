Architecture

Five Novel Innovations over Standard RAG

Each component is evaluated independently in the ablation study.

[I1] Query Intent Routing

Dynamic Retrieval Weight Adaptation

Classifies queries into four intents (Definitional, Procedural, Temporal, Comparative) and adjusts BM25/dense/temporal weight coefficients per query, replacing the fixed-weight RRF baseline.

+2.8% Context Precision
[I2] Semantic Authority Classification

SetFit Title-Level Classifier

A 118M-parameter SetFit classifier assigns authority tier (Official Gazette, Decree, Circular) from document titles only. Reduces false-positive rate from 44% to 29% versus full-text regex, with 91% fewer LLM authority calls.

+0.5% Context Precision
[I3] Temporal Supersession Detection

Cross-Year Conflict Surfacing

Cosine similarity on title embeddings identifies documents where a newer decree may override an older circular. Confirmed supersessions are flagged in the response; candidates are reported for review.

Legal safety contribution
[I4] Multi-Agent Legal Debate

Advocate + Devil's Advocate + Judge

Two debate agents argue competing interpretations of retrieved evidence before a Judge synthesises the final answer. Interpretive uncertainty is made explicit rather than collapsed into a single confident response.

+0.9% Faithfulness (largest single gain)
[I5] Token Cost Tracking

Per-Query Economic Accountability

Every Groq and Gemini call is metered at token level. Input/output tokens, per-model costs, and query total are returned with each response — enabling deployment cost planning and budget enforcement.

$0.0042 avg / query

Inference pipeline

From query to cited answer

Eight sequential stages. Each stage logs progress visible in real time via SSE streaming.

1

Language Detection

Arabic, French, English, or Algerian Darija (Arabic-script, 47-word lexicon with Levenshtein ≤ 2 fuzzy matching).

langdetect + Darija lexicon
2

Intent Classification + Weight Routing

One of four intents assigned; retrieval weights adjusted on the Delta-4 simplex accordingly.

Groq llama-3.3-70b
3

Query Expansion

Three alternative formulations generated to maximise corpus coverage across terminology variants.

Groq llama-3.3-70b
4

Hybrid Retrieval and RRF Fusion

BM25 and Gemini dense retrieval run in parallel. Results fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion (k=60). Adaptive IQR threshold triggers re-retrieval up to 3 iterations.

BM25 + Gemini text-embedding-004 → RRF
5

Semantic Authority Classification

SetFit classifier assigns authority tier to each retrieved chunk based on document title embedding.

SetFit + Gemini embeddings
6

Temporal Supersession Check

Newer documents checked against older ones for potential override. Confirmed conflicts surface as alerts.

Cosine similarity on title embeddings
7

Multi-Agent Legal Debate

Advocate and Devil's Advocate argue top-3 re-ranked chunks; Judge synthesises the final answer with inline citations.

Groq × 2 debate agents · Gemini Judge
8

Cost Accounting and Response Assembly

Token counts, per-model costs, confidence score, and citation chain assembled and returned.

Token-level metering
